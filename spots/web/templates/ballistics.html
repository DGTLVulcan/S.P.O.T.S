    This is the hold, not the dial. The two are opposites: a shot needing 4.3 mrad up on the turret needs the target sitting 4.3 mrad below the centre instead.

    The zoom ring changes the answer on a second focal plane scope. There the reticle keeps its size on the glass while the target grows behind it, so each mark is worth less angle the further you zoom in: a 4.4 mrad hold is 4.4 dots at the calibrated power and 2.2 at half of it. On a first focal plane scope the reticle grows with the image, the marks are worth the same at any power, and the hold never moves. Set which power your reticle is true at on the scope's equipment record — blank, top power is assumed. Every hold figure here is exact; only how much glass the picture shows is approximate, from a typical eyepiece.
